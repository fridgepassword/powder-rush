HOW TO PLAY
On the snowA D steer · S tuck (faster) · W brake · Space jump logs, rocks & crevasses
In the air← → spin · ↑ front flip · ↓ back flip
Hold WASD to grab — hold two for combo grabs (W+S Truck Driver, A+D Japan Air…)
Rails & jibsRide on from the start end · A D keep the balance meter centred · hold ←/→ to slide (×1.5) · Space pop off
LandingWatch the trick gauge: green = clean, yellow = sketchy save, red = bail. Easy saves close calls for you.
TouchHold left / right to steer (spins in the air) · hold the middle to tuck (grabs in the air) · swipe up to jump · in the air swipe up / down and hold to flip
ComboGates, tricks, grinds, hoops and close calls build your multiplier up to ×5. Keep chaining — it drains if you stop! Avalanche & One Life on Normal or Pro pay 2× for tricks off jumps and rails.
Power-upsMagnet pulls in snowflakes · Shield saves one crash · Boost rockets you downhill
On the slopesSmash snowmen for points (they slow you down), dodge bunnies and penguins, overtake rival skiers
SnowflakesCollect them and finish missions to earn snowflakes, then spend them in the Shop on outfits, skis, trees, snow colours, trails and weather
