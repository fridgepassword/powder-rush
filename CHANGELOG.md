# Changelog

Every update to Powder Rush gets a version number, shown under the logo on the home menu.

Version numbers go **MAJOR.MINOR.PATCH**:

- **MAJOR** goes up for a big overhaul of the whole game (2.0 rebuilt the graphics, park and menus).
- **MINOR** goes up when an update adds new things to play: a mode, a rival, new park features.
- **PATCH** goes up for fixes and small tweaks.

Each version is tagged in git (`v2.6.0` and so on), so any older version can be checked out.

## 2.6.0 (2026-11-11)

- S-Rail, Snake Rail, C-Box and S-Tube: curved jibs whose bends throw you outwards, so you have to lean into them. A LEAN arrow by the balance meter shows which way.
- New park features: Double Kink, A-Frame Box, Stair Rail beside a set of snow steps, Culvert Pipe and Rail Gardens (three jibs side by side).
- Rails, boxes and tubes are even longer, up to about 1,250 px.
- Superpipe sections are longer (170 to 220 m), with hoops above the walls to fly through.
- Fixed: flying through a Superpipe hoop didn't count.
- The version number now shows on the home menu.

## 2.5.0 (2026-11-11)

- The Superpipe is now its own area, reached only through purple pipe portals in the terrain park.
- Inside it you can ride one pipe after another for as long as you like. Teal portals between pipes take you back to the park.

## 2.4.0 (2026-11-11)

- Pipe portals that warp you into the Superpipe with a swirl effect.
- A MASSIVE jump at the end of every terrain park section.
- Much longer park rails, and balancing matters: the rail shoves you about, and staying centred earns a Perfect grind (+30%).
- Race: Normal (Bum) is easier. Impossible Luca now rides your pace, sends tricks the whole way and always wins by a hair. His Pro Kit is now won by finishing a race against him without crashing.
- Fixed: grabbing during a Space hop farmed huge scores.

## 2.3.2 (2026-11-11)

- Hard race mode now plays like Luca's old race: your twin races at his old pace with Normal landings.

## 2.3.1 (2026-11-11)

- Phones: the P-A-R-K letters and other top bars no longer cover the rider, the trick meter stays clear of them, and long tips wrap instead of running off screen.

## 2.3.0 (2026-11-11)

- Race mode has four rivals, one per difficulty: Jimmy (Easy), Bum (Normal), your twin in your own outfit (Hard) and Luca (Impossible), each with their own pace, tricks and reward.
- Best race times are kept per rival, and there's a new "Win a race against any rival" mission.

## 2.2.1 (2026-11-10)

- Much easier steering on phones: hold a side to turn, let go to straighten out. Keyboard steering is unchanged.

## 2.2.0 (2026-11-10)

- The Lodge: ride a 3-second chairlift to the summit, then pick your run from the trail signs.
- Race Luca mode: a countdown, then head to head to a finish line 1,000 m down.
- The Superpipe arrives in the terrain park.
- New shop items: buddies that follow you, trick effects, animated outfits and skis, a Mystery Gift, and gear you can only win.
- Removed the duck-under-branches feature.

## 2.1.0 (2026-11-10)

- Luca, a pro skier who passes you now and then and lands impossible tricks.
- Midnight Woods is now the hardest run, and the Terrain Park gets an orange Park rating.
- Fixed: jumps, trees and other features no longer overlap or land in gap zones.

## 2.0.0 (2026-11-10)

- Big overhaul: new trees, 3D kickers, rails and boxes, and park scenery (chairlift, DJ tent, fans).
- Park kickers from S up to XXL, longer rails, down rails, wave rails and picnic tables.
- Redesigned menu, HUD, pause, game over and shop screens.

## 1.5.0 (2026-11-09)

- Every mountain has its own snow texture.

## 1.4.1 (2026-11-09)

- Removed the boy rider's scarf, which looked like hair.

## 1.4.0 (2026-11-09)

- New girl rider with a braided ponytail. Pick boy or girl on the menu (G key).

## 1.3.0 (2026-11-09)

- Rebuilt rider model with jointed arms and legs, a backpack and goggles, and new poses for carving, tucking, braking, landing and each grab.

## 1.2.1 (2026-11-09)

- Deep Glades gets proper deep-powder ski tracks.

## 1.2.0 (2026-11-09)

- Upgraded graphics: redrawn trees, rider shadows, powder particles, sun rays and a frosted-glass HUD.
- The Pro avalanche is 15% faster.

## 1.1.0 (2026-11-09)

- Difficulty ratings on every map, and a special feature for each one: snowcat grooming, judges' zones, ice balls, snow bombs and headlamp lighting.
- Avalanche and One Life on Normal or Pro pay 2x for jump and rail tricks.
- A play-now link in the README.

## 1.0.0 (2026-11-09)

- First release: five mountains, Avalanche, Free Ride and One Life modes, three difficulties, flips, spins, grabs, rail grinds, power-ups, missions and a cosmetics shop.
